B2B Lead Generation Services

Build qualified, verified B2B prospect lists based on your Ideal Customer Profile — ready to import directly into your CRM and launch outbound campaigns.

What Is B2B Lead Generation?

B2B lead generation is the process of identifying and collecting contact information for businesses and decision-makers who match your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). Unlike B2C marketing, B2B lead generation focuses on company-level targeting — finding the right companies in the right industries, then identifying the specific people within those companies most likely to benefit from your product or service.

As a dedicated B2B lead generation specialist, I handle the entire research and verification process for you — from defining your ICP and sourcing prospects, to verifying every email and phone number before delivery. You receive a clean, CRM-ready spreadsheet you can immediately use for cold outreach, email campaigns, or sales development.

What You Receive

Decision-Maker Contacts

Name, job title, and LinkedIn profile of the specific person at each target company — CEOs, Founders, Sales Directors, Marketing Managers, and more.

Verified Business Emails

Every email address is verified using NeverBounce or Hunter.io to keep your bounce rate below 5% and protect your sender reputation.

Phone Numbers

Direct phone numbers where available, including mobile numbers sourced through ContactOut, Skrapp, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator.

Company Intelligence

Company name, website, industry, employee count, location, and any other data fields you specify for your ICP.

CRM-Ready Spreadsheets

All data delivered in a clean, formatted Google Sheet or Excel file — organized, deduplicated, and ready to import into HubSpot, Salesforce, or any CRM.

Revisions Included

If any contact doesn't match your ICP or a verified email bounces, I'll replace it — no questions asked.

My Lead Generation Process

01

ICP Discovery: Understand your target audience — industries, company sizes, geographies, and the job titles of decision-makers you want to reach.

02

Prospect Research: Using Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, and other tools, I identify companies and decision-makers matching your ICP.

03

Data Collection: Gather full contact details — email, phone, LinkedIn, company info — for each prospect.

04

Verification: Every email is verified for deliverability. Contacts with invalid emails are replaced to maintain list quality.

05

Delivery: You receive a clean, CRM-ready spreadsheet with all agreed data fields, on time and ready to use.
