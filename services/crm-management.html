CRM Data Management & Enrichment Services

A dirty CRM kills sales efficiency. I clean, enrich, and organize your contact database so your team spends time selling — not searching for the right phone number.

Why CRM Data Quality Matters

Your CRM is the foundation of your sales operation. Duplicate contacts, outdated emails, missing phone numbers, and inconsistent formatting are invisible drags on your sales team's productivity — and they lead to wasted outreach, bounced emails, and missed follow-ups.

As a CRM data management specialist, I audit your existing database, identify quality issues, and systematically clean, enrich, and organize your records. The result: a CRM your sales team can actually trust — with accurate contacts, complete data fields, and a structure that makes prospecting and follow-up fast and efficient.

CRM Services I Provide

Data Cleaning

Remove outdated, invalid, or irrelevant contacts. Standardize formatting for names, phone numbers, company names, and other fields across your entire database.

Duplicate Removal

Identify and merge duplicate contacts and company records to eliminate confusion and prevent your team from contacting the same prospect twice.

Data Enrichment

Fill in missing data fields — emails, phone numbers, job titles, LinkedIn URLs, and company information — using Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clearbit, and other enrichment tools.

Tagging & Segmentation

Apply consistent tags, custom properties, and list segmentation so your sales team can filter and target the right contacts instantly.

Email Verification

Verify all email addresses in your CRM using NeverBounce to remove hard bounces and protect your email sender reputation.

CRM Import & Setup

Import new lead lists into your CRM correctly — mapped to the right fields, tagged appropriately, and assigned to the right pipelines or sequences.
