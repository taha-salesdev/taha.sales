Hunter vs Snov.io: Which Email Finder Should You Use?

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  6 min read

Hunter.io and Snov.io often get compared because both started as domain-based email finders. Today, they've diverged in their capabilities. Here's a breakdown to help you pick the right tool for your B2B lead generation workflow.

Hunter.io: Accuracy Over Features

Hunter's main strength remains its original feature: Domain Search. You enter a company domain, and Hunter tells you the email pattern (e.g., first.last@) and lists verified emails it found on the web. It's incredibly accurate and easy to use.

  • Strengths: Highest accuracy for domain-based searches, great free tier (25 searches/month), clean interface.
  • Weaknesses: Lacks phone numbers and advanced sequencing capabilities.
  • Best for: PR outreach, one-off email finding, and teams prioritizing accuracy.

Snov.io: Budget All-in-One Outreach

Snov.io evolved into a multi-channel outreach platform. In addition to email finding and verification, it offers complex email drip campaigns, a CRM, and a LinkedIn prospecting extension. It's built for scale.

  • Strengths: Affordable pricing, great LinkedIn extension, powerful email drip campaigns.
  • Weaknesses: Email accuracy can occasionally be lower than Hunter's.
  • Best for: Solopreneurs, startups, and high-volume cold email campaigns on a budget.

Head-to-Head Comparison

Feature Hunter.io Snov.io
Domain Search Excellent Good
Email Verifier Yes (Very accurate) Yes
LinkedIn Extension No (Removed due to TOS) Yes (Very popular)
Cold Email tool Basic Advanced (Drip campaigns)

The Verdict

If you want a tool that does everything from finding leads on LinkedIn to sending automated follow-ups, Snov.io gives you more value for your money. If your primary need is finding and verifying emails with the highest possible accuracy and you already have an outreach tool, go with Hunter.io.
