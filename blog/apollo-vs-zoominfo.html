Apollo vs ZoomInfo: Which Is Better for B2B Prospecting?

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  7 min read

Apollo and ZoomInfo are two of the most widely used B2B prospecting platforms — but they're not the same tool. Understanding where each excels helps you choose the right one for your team's specific workflow and budget.

Apollo.io Overview

Apollo is an all-in-one sales intelligence and engagement platform. It combines a 275M+ contact database with email sequencing, dialer, CRM integrations, and analytics in a single product. It's designed to be a full-stack outbound tool — not just a data source.

  • Database size: 275M+ contacts
  • Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans from ~$49/month
  • Best for: SMBs, startups, and growth-stage companies

ZoomInfo Overview

ZoomInfo is an enterprise-grade intelligence platform with some of the deepest company and contact data available. It includes org charts, buying intent signals, technographic data, and verified direct dials. It's the go-to for enterprise sales teams with large budgets.

  • Database size: 321M+ contacts
  • Pricing: Starting ~$15,000/year (enterprise contracts)
  • Best for: Enterprise sales teams and large SDR organizations

Head-to-Head Comparison

Feature Apollo ZoomInfo
Email accuracy 85–92% 92–95%
Direct dials Good Excellent
Email sequences Built-in Available (Engage add-on)
Intent data Basic Advanced
Org charts Limited Full org chart data
Price $49–$99/month $15,000+/year

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Apollo if: You're a small to mid-size team, want an all-in-one outreach platform, and need solid data without enterprise pricing.

Choose ZoomInfo if: You're an enterprise team, need the deepest data quality and intent signals, have the budget, and prioritize data accuracy above all else.
