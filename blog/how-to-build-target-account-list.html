How to Build a Target Account List for B2B Outbound Sales

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  7 min read

A Target Account List (TAL) is the foundation of any account-based outbound strategy. Instead of casting a wide net, you identify specific high-fit companies and run coordinated, personalized outreach toward them. Here's how to build one that converts.

Step 1: Define Account-Level Selection Criteria

Your ICP defines the type of company. Your TAL is a specific list of companies that match it. Selection criteria for accounts typically include:

  • Industry and sub-vertical
  • Headcount range
  • Revenue range (if available)
  • Geography
  • Technology stack (e.g., uses Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
  • Funding stage or growth indicators

Step 2: Generate the Account Universe

Use Apollo, ZoomInfo, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator with your account-level filters to generate an initial list of matching companies. This is your universe — typically 500–2,000 companies depending on how niche your ICP is.

Step 3: Score and Prioritize

Not all accounts are equal. Prioritize your list based on fit score:

  • Tier 1: Perfect fit — top 50–100 accounts worth high-touch, highly personalized outreach
  • Tier 2: Strong fit — 100–300 accounts worth personalized sequences
  • Tier 3: Moderate fit — larger volume, lighter personalization

Step 4: Research Decision Makers at Each Account

For each company on your list, identify 1–3 decision makers with buying authority for your category. Collect their verified email and direct phone number. For Tier 1 accounts, add a personalization note for each contact.

Step 5: Assign to Reps and Launch

Assign accounts by territory, vertical, or rep preference. Track engagement at the account level — not just by individual contact — so you can see when multiple people from the same company have interacted with your outreach.
