How to Build a B2B Lead List for Free (With the Right Tools)

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  7 min read

Professional B2B lead databases are expensive. But if you're a startup founder, a solo consultant, or just testing a new offer, you don't need to spend $1,000+ a month on software. You can build a highly targeted, verified list of 100–200 prospects entirely for free by stacking the freemium tiers of the right tools.

Step 1: Identify Companies (Free LinkedIn)

Start with free LinkedIn (not Sales Navigator). Use boolean search in the main search bar to find companies in your target demographic. For example: "SaaS" AND "Series A" or "Marketing Agency" AND "New York". Build a spreadsheet of 50 target companies.

Step 2: Find the Decision Makers (Apollo Free Tier)

Apollo.io has the most generous free tier in the industry. It gives you 10,000 free monthly export credits (for basic info) and a small number of email credits. Better yet, sign up for an Apollo account, install the Chrome extension, and go to your target company's LinkedIn page. You can see the employees and filter by job title to find your decision maker.

Step 3: Get the Email Address (Hunter Free Tier)

If you run out of Apollo email credits, switch to Hunter.io. Hunter gives you 25 free searches per month. Go to your target company's website, click the Hunter Chrome extension, and it will show you the email pattern. Apply that pattern to the decision maker's name you found in Step 2.

Step 4: Verify the Email (ZeroBounce Free Tier)

Never skip verification, even on a free list. ZeroBounce offers 100 free email verifications per month. Upload your guessed emails to ensure they are valid before sending. This protects your domain reputation.

The Free Stack Summary

  • Company Research: LinkedIn (Free)
  • Contact Identification: Apollo.io (Free tier)
  • Email Finding: Hunter.io (25 free/mo) or Snov.io (50 free/mo)
  • Email Verification: ZeroBounce (100 free/mo)

The Trade-Off: Time

Building a list for free takes significantly more time than using paid tools. You are trading money for manual labor. This method works perfectly for highly targeted, low-volume campaigns (50–100 emails/week). Once your campaign starts generating revenue, take the first closed deal and invest it back into paid tools (like Apollo or Sales Navigator) to scale your time.
