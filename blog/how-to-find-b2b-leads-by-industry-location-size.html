How to Find B2B Leads by Industry, Location, Company Size, and Job Title

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  7 min read

The power of modern B2B prospecting tools is in their filters. Instead of generating generic lists, you can drill down to the exact type of company and buyer you want — by industry, location, size, and title — in minutes. Here's how to do it effectively.

The Four Core Filters

1. Industry

Industry filtering lets you target specific verticals where your product fits best. In Apollo, ZoomInfo, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator, you can filter by broad categories (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance) or drill into sub-industries (e.g., SaaS, Medical Devices, Fintech). Be specific — "Technology" returns millions of results; "B2B SaaS, 10–200 employees" returns a manageable, targeted pool.

2. Location

Location filters work at the country, state/region, and city level. For high-touch outreach, city-level targeting lets you build lists for local events or territory-specific campaigns. For remote-first products, country-level filtering by your target markets is usually sufficient.

3. Company Size

Company size (by headcount) is one of the most important filters for targeting correctly. Small businesses (1–50), mid-market (51–500), and enterprise (500+) have completely different buying processes, budget cycles, and decision-making structures. Filter to the range where your deal economics make sense.

4. Job Title

Job title filtering lets you target the exact decision-maker level you need. Best practices:

  • Use multiple title variations (e.g., "VP of Sales," "Head of Sales," "Director of Sales Development") to avoid missing relevant contacts
  • Filter by seniority level (Manager, Director, VP, C-Suite) in addition to specific titles
  • Avoid filtering too narrowly — a VP of Revenue is often the same buyer as a VP of Sales

Combining Filters for Precision

The real power comes from combining all four filters. Example: SaaS companies + US-based + 50–200 employees + VP of Marketing. This narrows millions of potential contacts to a precise, high-quality segment that matches your ICP exactly.

Which Tools to Use

  • Apollo.io: Excellent filtering across all four dimensions. Best value for most teams.
  • LinkedIn Sales Navigator: Superior for job title and seniority filtering. Great for relationship-based outreach.
  • ZoomInfo: Deepest company-level data, especially for headcount and revenue range filters.
