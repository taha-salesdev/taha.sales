How to Export Contacts From LinkedIn to Excel for B2B Sales

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  6 min read

You've found the perfect list of decision makers on LinkedIn or Sales Navigator. Now, how do you get them off LinkedIn and into an Excel sheet so you can actually email or call them? Here are the best ways to export LinkedIn contacts in 2026.

The Problem With LinkedIn's Native Export

LinkedIn does have a native tool to export your 1st-degree connections. (Go to Settings & Privacy > Data Privacy > Get a copy of your data > Connections). However, this is almost useless for B2B sales. Because of privacy changes years ago, LinkedIn no longer includes email addresses in this export unless the connection explicitly opted in (which almost no one does). You just get a list of names and titles.

Method 1: Use Apollo.io (Recommended)

This is the standard professional workflow:

  1. Install the Apollo Chrome Extension and log into your Apollo account.
  2. Run your search in LinkedIn or Sales Navigator.
  3. Click the Apollo extension overlay on the right side of the screen.
  4. Select the contacts you want (or select all on page).
  5. Click "Save." Apollo will find their verified business emails and add them to your Apollo list.
  6. Go to Apollo, open your saved list, click the export icon, and download as a CSV/Excel file.

Note: This finds their business email, not the personal email they used to sign up for LinkedIn.

Method 2: Use ContactOut

If you specifically need personal emails or mobile numbers (e.g., for recruiting or reaching hard-to-find executives), ContactOut is the better tool. The workflow is identical to Apollo: install the extension, view the profile on LinkedIn, click to reveal the contact info, save to a list, and export the list to CSV/Excel from the ContactOut dashboard.

Method 3: Bulk Scraping with PhantomBuster

If you have a massive Sales Navigator list (e.g., 1,000+ people) and don't want to click page by page, you can use a scraping automation tool like PhantomBuster. You feed it your Sales Navigator search URL, and the bot will visit every profile, scrape the name, title, and company data, and export it to an Excel sheet. You will then need to run that sheet through an email finder like Hunter.io to get the contact info.

Crucial Warning: LinkedIn Limits

LinkedIn aggressively monitors for scraping. If you use an extension to export hundreds of profiles in a single day, you risk getting your LinkedIn account restricted or permanently banned. Keep your daily extraction limits under 100 profiles per day on a standard account, or around 200–250 on a warmed-up Sales Navigator account.
