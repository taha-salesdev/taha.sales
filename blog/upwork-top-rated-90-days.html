3 Things I Did in My First 90 Days on Upwork to Get to Top Rated

August 19, 2026

Getting traction on Upwork as a new freelancer is notoriously difficult. When I first started offering lead generation and SDR services on the platform, I was competing against thousands of established profiles. Yet, within 90 days, I achieved the Top Rated badge with a 100% Job Success Score across multiple projects. Here are the three things that moved the needle.

1. Hyper-Personalized Proposals

Most freelancers copy-paste their cover letters. I treated Upwork proposals like B2B cold outreach. I researched the client's company, understood their specific ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) based on the job description, and offered a tiny "sample" of the work upfront—usually a list of 5-10 verified leads that matched their exact criteria. This proved my competence immediately.

2. Over-Communicating on Deliverables

Clients are terrified of freelancers going silent. For every project, I sent end-of-day summaries outlining exactly how many leads were generated, any blockers encountered, and the plan for the next day. This level of professional communication built immense trust and resulted in 5-star reviews.

3. Guaranteeing Data Quality

In the lead generation space, clients have been burned by bad data before. I made a hard promise in my profile and proposals: every email would be passed through NeverBounce, and any bounced emails would be replaced for free. By taking on the risk of bad data, I became the safe choice for clients.

If you're starting on Upwork, remember that you aren't just selling a skill—you are selling reliability.
