Best Contact Finder Extensions for Chrome in 2026

By Taha Bin Ishfaq  |  August 30, 2026  |  6 min read

A good Chrome extension turns LinkedIn and company websites into immediate sources of contact data. Instead of switching tabs to search a database, you extract the email and phone number right where you are. Here are the top extensions for 2026.

1. Apollo.io (Best Overall)

Apollo's extension works seamlessly over LinkedIn and Sales Navigator. When viewing a profile, you click the extension to reveal their email and phone number, and instantly add them to a sequence or export them to your CRM. It also works over company websites to show you all employees at that company.

  • Best for: Full-stack prospectors who want to add contacts directly to email campaigns.

2. ContactOut (Best for Personal Emails & Phones)

ContactOut specializes in finding contact info that isn't in standard B2B databases — specifically personal work emails and direct mobile numbers. It overlays directly on LinkedIn profiles. If Apollo can't find a direct dial, ContactOut usually can.

  • Best for: Recruiters and high-level enterprise sales reps who need direct mobile numbers.

3. Hunter.io (Best for Websites)

Because of LinkedIn's terms of service, Hunter removed its LinkedIn integration years ago. However, its website extension remains the best in the business. Click the Hunter icon while browsing any company's website, and it instantly reveals the email pattern they use and a list of verified employee emails.

  • Best for: People doing account-based research directly from company websites.

4. Lusha

Lusha provides a clean, fast overlay on LinkedIn profiles that reveals highly accurate B2B phone numbers and emails. It has a slightly better hit rate in European markets than some US-centric tools.

  • Best for: International calling campaigns.

5. Snov.io

Snov.io offers a very popular, affordable extension that works well on LinkedIn. It allows you to scrape entire pages of LinkedIn search results in bulk, rather than clicking one profile at a time.

  • Best for: High-volume, budget-conscious prospectors doing bulk LinkedIn scraping.
