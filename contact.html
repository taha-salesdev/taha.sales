Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you serve?

I work across a wide range of industries including Technology & SaaS, Healthcare, Real Estate, Finance, Insurance, Construction, Marketing Agencies, Education, Logistics, and Professional Services — with clients in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, and the Middle East.

What tools do you use for lead generation?

My primary tools include Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, ContactOut, Clearbit, and Skrapp for prospect research, and NeverBounce for email verification. I deliver all data as CRM-ready spreadsheets (Google Sheets or Excel).

How are leads verified?

Every email is verified using NeverBounce or Hunter.io before delivery to ensure a bounce rate below 5%. Phone numbers and LinkedIn profiles are manually checked. I do not deliver unverified or scraped data.

What is your typical turnaround time?

For standard lead generation projects (100–500 leads), turnaround is typically 2–4 business days. Larger CRM projects or market research engagements are scoped on a per-project basis. I provide daily or milestone-based progress updates.

Do you offer ongoing SDR or virtual assistant support?

Yes. I offer both project-based and ongoing monthly retainer arrangements for SDR support, CRM management, and virtual assistance. Contact me to discuss your specific requirements and we can build a custom engagement plan.
